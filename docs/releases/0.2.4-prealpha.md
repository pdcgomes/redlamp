Highlights:

- **Test Your Camera** (Help menu): the camera bench checks how your camera's raw files open, and compares Redlamp's rendering of each with the JPEG your camera saved inside it, on your Mac. It sends only the measurements, which you see first, to the cameras page on redlamp.app.
- **Right-click a photo in the filmstrip** to copy its settings or paste onto it without opening it. On a selected photo, the menu has the Copy, Paste and Sync items, acting on the selection.
- **Report This Problem** in the Camera Bench opens a bug report about that camera and raw mode, with its problems written in, for you to read and send.
