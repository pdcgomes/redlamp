Highlights:

- **Keep the filmstrip up**: turn off View › Filmstrip › Hide Automatically (or right-click the filmstrip, or Settings › Appearance), and the photo is fitted above the filmstrip instead of under it. The filmstrip also lost its scroll bar.
- **Skies that keep their branches**: a Sky mask's edit reaches only the sky's share of each pixel along its edge, so darkening a sky no longer leaves twigs and leaves pale against it. It applies to edits started from now on; photos already edited look as they did.
- **Generative fill on more Macs**: Macs with less than 16 GB of memory can download and try it, with a note that it hasn't been tested on them.
- **Hair and fine edges with ViTMatte**: a download in Settings › Models that adds the fine strands Subject, Background and People masks miss. Refine Edges now solves the edge again per pixel, and Feather and Edge keep a mask's stray hairs.
- **Quitting while AI masks are being prepared no longer crashes Redlamp**, a crash in Landscape masks since 0.2.0.
- **The Export dialog fits any window, and its settings scroll**, so the ones at the bottom can be reached; Report a Bug and Copy Settings fit too ([#290](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/290)).
- **The audit's second batch of fixes**: data safety, responsiveness, memory, concurrency and the render pipeline.
