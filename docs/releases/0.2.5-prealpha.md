Highlights:

- **Generative fill for removal** (Healing tool, Remove, Fill: Generative): an image model repaints what you remove when it's too large to fill from the photo, such as a parked car. It runs on your Mac after a 2.4 GB download, offers three fills a spot, labels them as generated, and needs 16 GB of memory or more.
- **Point Color** (Color Mixer, and in each mask): pick up to eight colours on the photo with an eyedropper, or take a mask's own, and set each one's Shift, Uniformity and Range, with Visualize Range to see what it reaches. The Even Skin Tone mask preset evens a face's colour while keeping its fine detail.
- **Curves inside masks**: each mask has an RGB curve and one each for red, green and blue, applied only as far as the mask reaches.
- **Reorder masks and components** by dragging, see a mask with the Image on B&W overlay, and set the overlay's Opacity.
- **Zoom and pan inside every tool**: the wheel, pinch and two-finger scroll work in Masking, Healing and Crop; Space-drag pans; [ and ] size the brush, with Shift for its feather.
- **More for masks and removal**: Feather and Edge sliders for AI masks and a Refine Edge brush, Objects masks by dragging a box or brushing, a Color swatch, Whites and Blacks that move each pixel's end points, Snow in Landscape masks, and a removed object's shadow and reflection removed with it.
