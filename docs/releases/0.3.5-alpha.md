Highlights:

- **Remove by drawing a box or brushing over it**: in the Healing tool with Click picks on Object, a drag picks what it draws a box around, as Segment Anything's box prompt, or what it brushes over, from up to eight points spread along the stroke, as the panel's new Drag menu says; it's the Objects mask's own Rectangle or Brush, one choice for both tools, and a click picks as before. A stroke that passes over the selected pick, or comes within the brush's width of its edge, adds what it brushes to that pick as one Add to Object step, such as a cup's handle a click left out; a stroke anywhere else picks something new. Picks make new Remove spots, so no existing edit renders differently ([#365](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/365)).
- **Sky offers the models it's better with**: on a Mac without Segment Anything 2.1 or Depth Anything 3, picking Sky in the mask picker or Create New Mask offers the missing ones in the Masks panel, with their size together (415.7 MB for both), Download and Not Now. Download fetches them and makes the mask with them; Not Now makes it from the estimate, which grows the sky down from the top of the frame and misses sky that branches, wires and buildings cut off, and Redlamp doesn't ask again on that Mac. Only picking Sky asks, and a photo with an iPhone's sky matte uses it instead. Settings › Models and the manual now say what Sky is made with, rather than that it uses models built into macOS.
- **Albums kept in Apple Photos**: File › Add Albums from Apple Photos…, until now Import from Apple Photos…, keeps the albums you choose in Photos by default, copying nothing. They appear in the Library panel's Apple Photos section after Folders, in Photos' own folders with their counts, and their photos join All Photographs, searches and smart collections, which `source:photos` and the filter bar's Source column tell apart. Thumbnails and previews come from Photos; an original kept only in iCloud is downloaded when it's needed (Develop, 1:1 in the Loupe, Compare and Survey, focus peaking, export, Copy to Folder and Download Originals, never for browsing), with progress and Cancel, into a cache kept within 20 GB by default (Settings › Library › Apple Photos). Redlamp keeps its edits, ratings, keywords and collections for these photos on this Mac; it follows Photos' changes at launch and while it runs, lists a photo deleted in Photos as missing in Library Health with its edit kept, and reads Photos' favourites as picks. Photo › Copy to Folder…, or an album's Copy to Folder…, copies photos into a folder with their Redlamp edits, metadata and history, each copy then standing in for its photo, with Undo Copy; Copy into a Folder, beside Keep in Photos, is 0.3.4's import as it shipped. Rename Photos… and Move to Folder… are refused for photos kept in Photos, with the reason, and Move to Trash offers Remove from Library instead. Photos is only read, never changed. It has been tried on a simulated library so far, not yet on a real one ([#495](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/495), [#500](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/500), [#501](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/501)).
