Highlights:

- **Sharper Fujifilm X-Trans photos**: Redlamp now develops X-Trans raws with Markesteijn's demosaic, ported from LibRaw, which resolves fine detail far better (MTF50 0.185 to 0.345 cycles per pixel on a sharp edge). It applies to every X-Trans photo, so existing edits of them look sharper too.
- **Value fields you can drag**: with the pointer over a number, a drag scrubs it across its range (Shift for finer steps), and a click still types. The color wheels show their hue, saturation and luminance, and the tone curve its point's input and output, as values you can type or scrub.
- **Auto Settings keeps bright skies from clipping**: Highlights holds the brightest 1% of the photo, and the exposure gives way, by up to 1 EV, where Highlights can't ([#325](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/325)).
- **Fixes**: the filmstrip slides away and back where it was left, not at the first photo ([#344](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/344)); Choose… in the Export dialog's Export to opens the folder panel on the first click ([#356](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/356)); the Diagonal Split's knob drags the split instead of zooming the photo ([#326](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/326)); and the download opens without Gatekeeper refusing it ([#333](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/333)).
- **Duplicate and Invert** now stores the copy's inversion with the mask, so a copy made in 0.2.7 opens un-inverted in 0.2.6 and earlier.
- **More of the audit's fixes**: responsiveness, render pipeline and lean code.
