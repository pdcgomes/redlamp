Highlights:

- **A new Masks panel**: one picker for every mask, behind New Mask, Add, Subtract and Intersect; every action on screen, with a menu button on each mask and component; and masks that preview themselves, with a thumbnail of each one's coverage and its overlay while the pointer is over it. Invert works for a whole mask, and the People picker chooses people and their parts. A mask preset now goes to every selected photo, and the Landscape picker offers the regions it finds, as one mask or one each ([#301](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/301) to [#307](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/307)).
- **Panel switches**: an eye on each Develop panel's header switches the panel off, so its settings render neutral, as a step in History; an Edited chip says how many of its settings have changed and lists them when the pointer is over it ([#317](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/317)).
- **Clipped highlights keep their colour**: pulled below white by Highlights or Exposure, highlights the camera clipped in one or two colours keep the colour around them instead of turning lilac with a cyan band, and areas clipped in every colour fade smoothly to neutral. It applies to new edits; existing edits look as they did ([#369](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/369)).
- **More cameras**: Nikon's High Efficiency NEFs (HE and HE\*) open, through Redlamp's own fork of LibRaw, and the Sony A7 V opens and the A1 II no longer shows a strip along the bottom ([#168](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/168), [#169](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/169)).
