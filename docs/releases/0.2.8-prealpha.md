Highlights:

- **Redlamp Reproduction**: a Base Look with no tone curve, for copying artwork and documents. Once the exposure is anchored to the camera, a grey scale's patches render at their own lightness ([#360](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/360)). **Calibrate from Target** sets the anchor from a click on a target's grey patch, for the camera or for one photo ([#359](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/359)).
- **The histogram's readout**: with the pointer over the photo, the line under the histogram shows the R, G and B percentages or L\*a\*b\*, and in stops under Redlamp Reproduction ([#319](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/319)).
- **The photo takes the panels' room**: hiding the side panels with Tab, F7 or F8 fits the photo to the space they leave, rendered again at its new size ([#358](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/358)).
- **Fixes**: a mask chosen by its pin no longer keeps its overlay once another mask is selected ([#364](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/364)); H and Show Spots hide the Healing tool's spots, so a pick's fill can be judged with nothing over it ([#336](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/336)); and each Color Grading wheel's Hue and Saturation values keep apart, so Hue 30 and Saturation 10 no longer read "30S 10".
- **A disk image to download**: the site's download is a disk image, with Redlamp beside a link to Applications; updates and Homebrew keep the zip ([#333](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/333)).
