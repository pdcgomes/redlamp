Not posted yet. Attach `card.mp4` (LinkedIn plays it as a video) or `card.png`. 171 words:

```text
I've been building Redlamp, a free, open-source raw photo editor for the Mac, mostly with AI agents in Cursor.

Earlier this week I started on its library: ratings, flags, keywords, collections and search, meant to stay fast with hundreds of thousands of photos. It's the largest piece of work in the project so far, and by the end of the first day eleven agents had worked on it. None of their chats had the whole picture.

So the plan lives outside the chats. Every piece of work is a row in a tracker with its own GitHub issue, and a board in Cursor shows what's being built, what's been measured and what's waiting on me. I can step away and come back without reading any transcripts.

I wrote up how it works. The board is packaged as a Cursor skill, if you'd like to try it on your own project:
https://redlamp.app/blog/how-i-run-a-big-feature

If you run a lot of agents on one codebase, I'd love to hear how you keep track of them.
```
