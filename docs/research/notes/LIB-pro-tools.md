# Professional browsing, culling and ingest tools

Research for the library study (LIB-01), written on 5 October 2026: how Capture One (16.0 to 16.8, 2022 to 2026), Photo Mechanic (6 in 2019 to 2026.6, and Plus until February 2026), Adobe Bridge (15.x and 16.x, 2024 to 2026) and FastRawViewer (2.0.11, manual of 27 March 2026) organise, cull, search, rename and ingest photos, how they scale, and what users complain about.

Capture One's help-centre pages refuse scripted requests, so their text was read through the same site's public help-centre API, as in [TC-capture-one-teardown.md](TC-capture-one-teardown.md); links go to the articles. Adobe's help site couldn't be reached, so Bridge facts come from Adobe's community, an Adobe evangelist's blog and other vendors' manuals. Forum links are dated by their first post.

## 1. Summary

- Culling feels instant because nothing is rendered: Photo Mechanic and Capture One's Cull view show the raw's embedded JPEG ([2026](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=17188.0), [Culling images](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/7185822431645)).
- So the screen shows the camera's JPEG, not the edit: Capture One's Cull view can't show adjustments ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/11148235628829)), and FastRawViewer's grid ignores its own ([2024](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/1123)).
- The disk sets the pace, and NAS units handle small scattered reads badly ([2022](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=15255.0)); of the four, only FastRawViewer tunes parallel reads per volume ([manual, p. 146](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=146)).
- Work that needs every file's metadata blocks the view: Photo Mechanic locks a contact sheet to gather sort data ([2020](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=13301.0)), and users report Capture One scanning a whole card before it excludes duplicates ([2024](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/16125381142813)).
- Capture One says catalogs of tens of thousands of photos may slow down and can't be searched together ([splitting catalogs](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/30363721859741)); a user sees 12-second freezes after a filter at 28,000 photos ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/9826370590109)).
- Photo Mechanic Plus's catalog was withdrawn on 27 February 2026 after the third-party technology under it became unstable on current systems ([Camera Bits](https://home.camerabits.com/photo-mechanic-plus-is-no-longer-available-for-purchase/)).
- Undo is missing where culling happens: in Photo Mechanic ([2019 to 2025](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=12461.0)), Capture One's Cull view ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/10060182640541)) and Bridge's Batch Rename ([2019](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/add-undo-batch-rename-170434)).
- Colour labels don't travel: names differ by app and language, Capture One maps tags to IPTC Urgency and recognises English names only ([Filters tool](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002526358)), and Lightroom's pick flag isn't in the XMP specification ([2025](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/1226)).
- Renames and moves made in Finder break Capture One catalogs ([2024](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/17053816081565)), Photo Mechanic Plus catalogs ([moved folders](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001164434)) and Bridge collections ([2019](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/correct-way-to-migrate-collections-170482)).
- Photo Mechanic culls with single keys, FastRawViewer offers keymaps imitating Lightroom, Bridge and Photo Mechanic ([keymaps](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/keymaps/)), and Bridge rates with a modifier by default ([Kost, 2017](https://jkost.com/blog/2017/10/20-shortcuts-for-working-with-adobe-bridge.html)).
- Photo Mechanic's Ingest is the model for import ([Ingest](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000207409)); of the manuals reached, only FastRawViewer's describes verifying copies ([manual, p. 47](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=47)).
- Photo Mechanic Plus's search syntax (named fields, logic, empty fields, relative dates, distance from a point) maps onto LIB-06's query language ([Search](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001077445)).

## 2. Capture One

**Organisation.** A Session is a folder with Capture, Selects, Output and Trash subfolders and a `.cosessiondb` holding albums and favourites. Viewing photos in a session creates a `CaptureOne` subfolder beside them with `Cache/Proxies` (`.cop` previews, `.cof` focus masks), `Cache/Thumbnails` (`.cot`) and `Settings###` (`.cos` adjustments, `.comask` masks); the settings move with the folder ([Sessions guide](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003115137), [CaptureOne folder](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002862137), [Cache](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/30111725200413)). A Catalog is a database package with its cache inside, referencing or holding originals, organised by Albums, Smart Albums, Projects (which bound a search) and Groups (which nest) ([Sessions vs Catalogs](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/30041173920029), [Catalogs guide](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003108698)). Pairing RAW and JPEG only gives them one name ([pairing](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/30110560619165)); stacking is a 77-vote request ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/11048240325021)).

**Browsing and culling.** Since 16.0 (November 2022) the importer and the Cull view browse from embedded previews, group similar photos, rate, tag, filter and zoom; Face Focus (16.2) zooms to faces or eyes ([16.0 notes](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/7619202722589), [Culling images](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/7185822431645), [Cull View](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/8265235276957)). Select Next When advances after a rating or tag ([Rating and tagging](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002743718)). Assisted Review (16.8 beta, May 2026) tags closed eyes, missed focus and bad exposure ([Assisted Review](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/35841394167837)). Shortcuts come in reassignable sets ([hotkeys](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003399778)), and 2023 requests ask for a reject key and for keywords in the Cull view ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/11213556068637), [2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/10964124899869)) (unverified for 16.8).

**Search and keywords.** The Filters tool counts each rating and colour and takes added metadata filters, and a search can be saved as a smart album ([Filters tool](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002526358)); no search spans catalogs ([Catalogs guide](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003108698)). Each document has a keyword library, shared `.cokeywordsdb` libraries load into every document, and lists import from Lightroom's text export ([keyword library](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002544378), [managing](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002545577)); hierarchies are typed with `|` or `>` ([hierarchical keywords](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002544338)).

**Import and renaming.** The importer copies or references, makes subfolders from tokens, backs up to a second place, excludes duplicates, applies metadata and styles, and ejects or erases the card ([importing](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002492617), [backup](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003112938)); renaming is off when photos are indexed in place ([network catalogs](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002519778)). Tokens are shared by import, capture, rename and export, and Batch Rename adds find and replace ([tokens](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002578618), [find and replace](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002530097)). No copy verification is documented (unverified).

**Interop.** Capture One reads embedded EXIF, IPTC-IIM and XMP and `.xmp` sidecars and writes only sidecars; Auto Sync Sidecar XMP is None, Load or Full Sync, the last slow on large new imports ([XMP sidecars](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002544898), [Image settings](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002484457)). Sidecars carry ratings, colour tags, keywords and IPTC, edits stay in `.cos` files or the catalog, and `name.NEF` and `name.jpg` share one `name.xmp` ([XMP sidecars](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002544898), [pairing](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/30110560619165), [CaptureOne folder](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002862137)). The Urgency link became switchable in 16.5.6 (February 2025), which also began reading other apps' XMP crop and rotation ([16.5.6 notes](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/25229037172765)). Lightroom catalogs arrive with flattened keywords and without smart collections ([switching FAQ](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003692438)).

**Scale and performance.** Capture One wants catalogs and sessions on local disks: NAS set-ups are outside its support, editing should happen on a local copy, and cloud sync must be paused ([Sessions vs Catalogs](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/30041173920029)); a dropped external or NAS volume can leave a SQLite `.journal` that stops a document opening ([database error](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/30493722534941)). Previews rebuild when a capture date lies in the future or timestamps disagree ([regenerating previews](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002800517)). A catalog has one writer ([sharing](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002518297)), 16.8.2 added LAN multi-user sessions in beta ([multi-user](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/36870089862813)), and each feature release upgrades documents one way ([16.8 notes](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/articles/35747427882653)).

**Praise and complaints.** Users credit 16.x's grouping with cutting culling time ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/11213556068637), [2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/10964124899869)). A 52-vote request asks for faster file and DAM work, above all on network drives, and a commenter quotes support in December 2023 as putting no resources into large-catalog performance ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/9826370590109)). Others report freezes on search and keyword edits even after splitting the catalog, Load Metadata on 15,000 files estimating 72 hours, and Synchronize recounting files it already knows ([2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/9816833103645), [2023](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/10359022328861), [2024](https://support.captureone.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/16155812593949)).

## 3. Photo Mechanic and Photo Mechanic Plus

**Organisation.** Standard Photo Mechanic has no database: a contact sheet is a tab of one or more folders beside Navigator and Favorites panels ([contact sheets](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000207551)), and metadata goes into files or sidecars at once ([2025](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=12461.0)). Plus (2020) added catalogs across drives with proxies for offline photos ([catalogs](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001078324)). Plus left sale on 27 February 2026, and Photo Mechanic 6 and Plus, being Intel builds, stop running when macOS 28 drops Rosetta ([Camera Bits](https://home.camerabits.com/photo-mechanic-plus-is-no-longer-available-for-purchase/), [July 2026](https://home.camerabits.com/photo-mechanic-6-on-mac-its-time-to-plan-ahead/)).

**Browsing and culling.** Opening a raw reads mostly EXIF and makernotes (usually under 64 KB) and the embedded JPEG (a few MB), never most of the file ([2023](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=15777.0)). Raw rendering (Apple ImageIO or Adobe DNG Converter) is optional, on demand (Q) or only when the embedded preview is small ([render cache](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001146201), [2026](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=17188.0)); 100% views render only on zoom ([2024](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=16307.0)). The disk cache stays small and can be emptied on quit because previews regenerate quickly ([caching](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001146200)). Held arrow keys move at the system key-repeat rate ([slow rendering](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000828176)); space opens a preview with 2-up comparison ([preview](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001144384), [shortcuts](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000317772)).

**Tags, colour classes and keys.** T toggles a tag; eight colour classes with editable names and colours, and presets matching Lightroom, sit on the number keys; stars use Control-1 to 5 unless swapped ([tagging](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001142232), [colour classes](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001142942), [stars](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001143067)). Most shortcuts became customisable in the Apple-silicon builds ([What's new](https://home.camerabits.com/whats-new-in-photo-mechanic/), [2026.6](https://home.camerabits.com/photo-mechanic-update-with-new-custom-keyboard-shortcuts-avif-support-and-more/)).

**Metadata.** The Metadata (IPTC) Template, formerly the IPTC Stationery Pad, applies only ticked fields, appends or prefixes, saves snapshots or `.XMP` files and reverse-geocodes places ([template](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000207623)). Code replacements expand `=code=` from tab-separated files ([code replacements](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000223660)), and variables take substring, case and translate modifiers, as in `{caption:0:lc:tr: /_}` ([variables](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000207639), [substrings](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001077381)).

**Ingest and renaming.** Ingest (⌘G) copies several cards at once to a primary and a secondary destination, names folders from variables (`/Pictures/{year4}/{datesortdash} - Event/`), skips photos already ingested on this computer, can start on card insertion, applies a template, renames with a sequence, opens the contact sheet as files land, and unmounts or erases the card ([Ingest](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000207409)); clock changes can confuse the skipping ([known issues](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001248753)). Rename (⌘M) and Copy/Move (⌘Y) share the variables and a collision policy, without undo ([rename](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001141415), [copy/move](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000223598), [files](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001145359), [2019](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=12038.0)). No copy verification is documented (unverified).

**Search (Plus).** Queries span the active catalogs and support named fields, AND/OR/NOT, `is empty`, comparisons, relative dates (`> 3 months ago`), `within 6.5mi of` a point, anchors and case toggles ([Search](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001077445)). Catalog Sync is manual and files new, missing and offline photos into collections ([Catalog Sync](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001161171)).

**Interop.** Raws get XMP sidecars and are left untouched by default; JPEG, TIFF and PSD get embedded IPTC and XMP; structured keywords can go to XMP's hierarchical field ([IPTC/XMP](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001146198)). Lightroom catalogs can't be read; Lightroom must write XMP first ([Lightroom](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001164127)).

**Scale and performance.** Camera Bits doesn't expect to improve NAS browsing ([2022](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=15255.0)). Filters and custom sorts load every photo's metadata first, even in Plus ([2020](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=13301.0), [2023](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=15913.0)), and these preloads still stall on large selections ([known issues](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001248753)). Plus reads online originals instead of its proxies, so a mounted NAS slows catalog browsing; catalogs must stay off a NAS and are single-user ([2024](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=16059.0), [2024](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=16484.0), [storage](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001159651), [sharing](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001159649)).

**Praise and complaints.** Testimonials Camera Bits chose praise ingest to two places and captioning on deadline ([tour](https://home.camerabits.com/tour-photo-mechanic/)). Complaints: no undo ([2025](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=17018.0)), NAS speed ([2020](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=13185.0), [2024](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=16150.0)), unused proxies ([2021 to 2024](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=14400.0)), the 2023 move to subscriptions ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2023/11/15/photo-mechanic-caves-to-industry-trends-shifts-to-subscription-model/); a perpetual licence is now sold too, [pricing](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48001252734)), and ingest stalling at 10,000 photos a day, traced to exFAT on Windows ([2025](https://forums.camerabits.com/index.php?topic=17080.0)).

## 4. Adobe Bridge

**Organisation.** Bridge browses folders with a cache and a search index, not a catalog; Find sees only folders already indexed ([2018](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-search-multiple-folders-for-keywords-170016)). Collections (`.filelist`) and smart collections (`.collection`) are files in Bridge's support folder with fixed paths, so moving photos to a new drive orphans them ([2020](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/where-does-bridge-store-preferences-and-all-collections-information-171475), [2019](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/correct-way-to-migrate-collections-170482)).

**Browsing, culling and keys.** Space opens Full Screen Preview and ⌘B Review mode, where 0 to 9 rate and label; elsewhere ratings take ⌘0–5 and labels ⌘6–9 unless Preferences > Labels drops the modifier, and ⌥⌫ rejects ([Kost, 2017](https://jkost.com/blog/2017/10/20-shortcuts-for-working-with-adobe-bridge.html); unverified for 16.x). Purple has no default key ([2023](https://community.adobe.com/bug-reports-557/p-bridge-label-colours-keyboard-shortcut-options-1501472)); a shortcut editor came with the 14.0 beta in August 2023 ([editor](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/keyboard-shortcut-customization-workflows-in-adobe-bridge-beta-14-0-0-57-176530)). 15.0 (October 2024) and 15.1 (June 2025) fixed slow scrolling, arrow-key lag and slow previews ([15.0](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-556/adobe-bridge-15-0-max-release-now-live-177819), [15.1 beta](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-556/adobe-bridge-beta-15-1-is-now-available-178187), [15.1](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-556/adobe-bridge-15-1-is-now-available-178221)).

**Filters and smart collections.** The Filter panel shows only values present in the folder and ORs values within a category; AND needs Find ([2020](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-filters-not-showing-172644), [2019](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/filtering-keywords-by-eliminating-170551)). Smart collections recheck their folders when opened ([2017](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-re-indexes-smart-collections-178362)).

**Batch Rename.** ⇧⌘R builds names from parts such as text and sequence numbers, and String Substitution accepts regular expressions with capture groups ([Kost](https://jkost.com/blog/2017/10/20-shortcuts-for-working-with-adobe-bridge.html), [2018](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/adobe-bridge-batch-rename-out-of-order-179191), [2024](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/batch-rename-using-string-substitution-in-bridge-177762), [2024](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/adobe-bridge-batch-rename-expression-help-177687)). There's no undo, only the option to keep the current name in XMP before renaming ([2019](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/add-undo-batch-rename-170434)); whether the dialog previews every new name is unverified. Photo Downloader imports cards; 13.0.2 couldn't see them until 13.0.3 (February 2023) ([2023](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/adobe-bridge-get-photos-from-camera-card-175940)).

**Interop.** Changes to raws go to sidecars, JPEGs use embedded XMP only, and reject is rating −1 ([FastRawViewer manual, pp. 53, 54, 58](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=53)). Rating in Bridge a photo that Lightroom also catalogs raises a metadata conflict in Lightroom ([2017](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/how-do-i-import-star-ratings-from-lightroom-to-bridge-178251)).

**Cache and scale.** A central cache, optionally exported into each folder, purges stale items after 30 days by default, and a Camera Raw update can invalidate every thumbnail ([2017](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-2018-re-caching-all-thumbnails-again-178740), [2017](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/advice-on-purging-the-central-cache-in-bridge-178399), [2018](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-loses-its-index-when-closed-169920)). Users report minutes to list 9,100 photos (2017), half a second to several seconds per photo when culling folders of about 1,000 raws (2022) and 16.0.3 unusable (2026) ([2017](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-performance-poor-178594), [2022](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-2022-very-slow-174115), [2022](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/adobe-bridge-2023-very-slow-for-sorting-culling-images-175251), [2026](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/whew-adobe-bridge-2026-v-16-03-21-is-unusable-1559675)). On networks, NAS searches came back empty until each folder was browsed, a 90,000-photo index rebuilt at every launch, and metadata writes to older Windows shares froze Macs ([2017](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/searching-nas-drives-in-bridge-178639), [2018](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-loses-its-index-when-closed-169920), [2018](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/bridge-crashes-labelling-rating-writing-metadata-on-windows-share-179279)).

**Praise and complaints.** Long-time users recall rating three to five photos a second in older versions and dragging in a folder to tag it, and blame regressions since Bridge 2022 ([2022](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/adobe-bridge-2023-very-slow-for-sorting-culling-images-175251), [2026](https://community.adobe.com/questions-558/whew-adobe-bridge-2026-v-16-03-21-is-unusable-1559675)).

## 5. FastRawViewer

**Organisation and culling.** LibRaw's folder viewer has no catalog. Its single view decodes the raw; J switches to the embedded or a paired JPEG, and the status bar shows which exist ([home](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/), [manual, p. 108](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=108)); grid thumbnails are the embedded previews ([2024](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/1123)). The exposure tools read raw data: a histogram with EV0 three stops below saturation, per-channel clipping percentages, overlays coloured by clipped channel (O) and an underexposure limit of 8 EV by default ([manual, pp. 119 to 125](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=119)). Rejected photos move to a `_Rejected` subfolder, since cards and shares have no Trash ([manual, p. 49](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=49)). A Sort & Filter panel filters a folder by rating, label and filename ([manual, p. 60](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=60)).

**Keys.** Alt-1–5 rate, Alt-6–9 label and Alt-0 clears ([manual, p. 58](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=58)); ⇧⌘⌫ rejects, C and M copy and move, and ⌘Z undoes them ([how-to](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/howtos/sorting-photos)); every key and mouse button can be reassigned ([manual, p. 239](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=239)).

**XMP.** Hand-made changes to ratings, labels, title, description, orientation, white balance and exposure go to sidecars; label names follow Bridge, Lightroom or custom styles; reject (−1) and `photoshop:Urgency` for Capture One are options; a shared sidecar name raises a warning; other apps' edits appear only after a reload ([manual, pp. 53 to 56](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=53)). As Lightroom ignores sidecars on cards and for JPEGs, LibRaw advises copying chosen photos to disk before rating ([manual, p. 56](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=56), [how-to](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/howtos/sorting-photos)).

**Renaming and copying.** Rename tokens sit between slashes (`/Filename/`) with a before-and-after sample; copies and moves bring paired files along and can be verified ([manual, pp. 47, 78](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=78)).

**Scale and performance.** LibRaw likens gigabit network storage to a local hard disk and promises nothing for Wi-Fi ([manual, p. 15](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=15)). It suggests 1 to 3 parallel reads on hard disks, 2 to 3 on gigabit NAS and 4 to 6 on fast cards, set per media type or volume, and prefetches further in the direction of travel ([pp. 146, 206, 211](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=146)). Its SQLite cache keeps only thumbnails that were slow to make ([p. 41](https://updates.fastrawviewer.com/data/FastRawViewer2-Manual-ENG.pdf#page=41)). On Windows an offline mapped drive delays start-up by up to a minute ([2025](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/1222)).

**Praise and complaints.** A user leaving Bridge found it quick even on network shares ([2025](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/1136)). Complaints: auto-advance after a reject sometimes going backwards ([2015 to 2026](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/175)), no keywords ([2026](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/1262)) and thumbnails ignoring its edits ([2024](https://www.fastrawviewer.com/node/1123)).
