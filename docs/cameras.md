# Cameras

Redlamp opens raw files with [LibRaw](https://www.libraw.org), an open-source library that reads the formats of more than a thousand cameras. Redlamp uses it only to unpack the sensor data and the file's metadata; black levels, white balance, demosaicing, highlight reconstruction, colour and everything after are Redlamp's own, on the GPU. LibRaw is vendored as a pinned build (`config/vendored-libs.json`) and used under the CDDL-1.0, one of its two licences. This list is published at [redlamp.app/cameras](https://redlamp.app/cameras).

What the lists mean:

- **Verified:** a CC0 sample from [raw.pixls.us](https://raw.pixls.us) is in Redlamp's decode tests (`tests/decode/cameras.json`), which check its layout, crop, black and white levels, white balance, colour matrix, orientation and sensor data on every test run. A colour reference means its default rendering is also compared with a recorded one (CIEDE2000).
- **In an evaluation set:** a CC0 sample is developed in Redlamp's look-development set or its dust evaluation, but its decoding isn't checked field by field.
- **Supported by LibRaw:** LibRaw reads the camera's files, with any limits LibRaw notes beside it. Redlamp hasn't checked each camera, and one without a sample may have quirks no test has caught, in its colour, crop or levels for example. Cameras released after this LibRaw version need a LibRaw update.

Is your camera missing from the verified list? Upload a CC0 sample to [raw.pixls.us](https://raw.pixls.us) and [open an issue](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/new) naming it.
