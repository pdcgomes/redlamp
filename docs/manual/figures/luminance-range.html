title: The Luminance Range bar tag: Range caption: **Four handles shape the band of tones a Luminance Range selects.** The tint above the bar is how much of each tone is selected: fully between the inner handles, fading to nothing at the outer ones. The four values under the bar say where each handle is; drag one, or type it, to move its handle. Depth Range's bar works the same way, from Far to Near. ---
