Right-click or Control-click another photo in the filmstrip to copy its settings or paste onto it, and the one you're editing stays open. Pasting runs in the background, and recomputes the pasted AI masks for that photo. Undo Sync Settings takes it back.

On a selected photo, the menu has the Photo menu's Copy, Paste and Sync items, with their keys, and they act on the selection as they do there.
