Generative fill was offered only on Macs with 16 GB of memory or more, the most it has been tested on. Now every Mac can turn it on: in the Healing tool, choose Remove and set Fill to Generative.

On a Mac with less memory, Redlamp says before the download that it hasn't been tested there, and it may be slow or not work. If a fill fails, its message says so too.
