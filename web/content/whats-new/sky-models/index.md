On a Mac without Segment Anything 2.1 and Depth Anything 3, picking Sky now offers them in the Masks panel, a 415.7 MB download together. With them, a Sky mask takes in the sky that branches, wires and buildings cut off. Without them, Sky is estimated from the photo's colours, grown down from the top of the frame, so it misses sky that's enclosed.

Download fetches them and makes the mask; Not Now makes it from the estimate, and Redlamp doesn't ask again on that Mac. The models run on your Mac, and your photos are never uploaded.
