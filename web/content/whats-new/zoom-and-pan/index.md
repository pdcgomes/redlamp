With Masking, Healing or Crop open, the wheel, a pinch and a two-finger scroll zoom and pan the photo as they do everywhere else. Hold Space and drag to pan while a tool is active.

[ and ] size the brush, and Shift with them its feather; ⌘-scroll sizes it from the pointer. Its ring shows the size as it changes, also while you drag its slider.
