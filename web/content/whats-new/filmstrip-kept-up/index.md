The filmstrip slides away while you edit, and comes back when the pointer reaches the bottom of the window. To keep it up, turn off View › Filmstrip › Hide Automatically, or right-click the filmstrip beside its photos, or use Settings › Appearance.

With it kept up, the photo is fitted above the filmstrip, so nothing of it is hidden. F6 still hides the filmstrip, and gives its room back to the photo. The strip also lost its scroll bar: it scrolls with the wheel, the trackpad and the arrow keys.
