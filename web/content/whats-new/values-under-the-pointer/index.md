While the pointer is over the photo, the line under the histogram shows the R, G and B percentages there, in Display P3. Show L\*a\*b\* Values, in the histogram's right-click menu, the View menu and ⌘K, shows L\*a\*b\* relative to D50 instead. The values come from the render without its overlays, averaged over 5 × 5 pixels. Under Redlamp Reproduction the line adds the exposure in stops from the anchored 18% grey, marked ≈ while the photo isn't calibrated.

In the picture, a chart's middle grey once calibrated from it, the pointer just out of frame: L\* 50.9 against 50.87.
