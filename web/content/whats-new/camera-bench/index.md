Help › Test Your Camera… checks how Redlamp opens your camera's raw files, and compares its rendering of each with the JPEG your camera saved inside it. It groups your photos by camera and raw mode, and shows the two side by side.

Your photos stay on this Mac. When you choose to, it sends only the measurements, which you see first, to add to the [cameras page](https://redlamp.app/cameras).
