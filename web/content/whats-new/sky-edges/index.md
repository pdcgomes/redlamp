summary: Darken a sky, and the twigs and leaves against it stay as they were.

A pixel along a sky's edge is often part sky and part branch. A Sky mask's edit used to apply to the whole pixel, as far as the mask covered it, so darkening the sky left twigs and leaves pale against it. Now the edit reaches only the sky's share of each such pixel, and the branch keeps its own colour.

It applies to Sky masks in edits you start from now on. Photos you've already edited look as they did.
