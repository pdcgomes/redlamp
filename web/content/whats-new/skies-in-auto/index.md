title: Auto Settings keeps bright skies
summary: Auto no longer brightens a shaded photo until the sky above it turns white.
image: castle.jpg
imageAlt: A castle on a wooded hill under a cloudy sky after Auto Settings, its clouds faint and washed out in 0.2.6 on the left and kept in 0.2.7 on the right
---

Auto Settings (⌘U) set the exposure from the photo's average brightness, so a photo mostly in shade could be brightened until a bright sky above it turned white. Now Highlights holds back the brightest part of the photo, and where Highlights alone can't keep it from clipping, the exposure gives way, by up to a stop. In the picture, Auto on a drone's photo: 0.2.6 on the left, 0.2.7 on the right.

Photos you've already edited keep their settings; press Auto again for the new ones.
