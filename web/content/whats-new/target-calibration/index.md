Under Redlamp Reproduction, choose Calibrate from Target, under the Base Look or in the Photo menu, and click a target's grey patch in a shot exposed as the meter read and white-balanced on it. Type the patch's reference L\* in the popover: the button named after the camera keeps a calibration for it and sets Exposure to 0, and Set This Photo's Exposure changes only this photo. Each edit under the look carries its camera's exposure anchor, and the line under the Base Look says whether the camera is calibrated.

In the picture, the popover over a ColorChecker's middle grey patch.
