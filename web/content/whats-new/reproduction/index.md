Redlamp Reproduction, a Base Look for copying artwork and documents, has no tone curve: once exposure is anchored, each patch of a target's grey scale renders at its own L\*, and anything brighter than white turns white. It's in its own section at the bottom of the Base Look menu, and edits with other looks render as before.

In the picture, a ColorChecker shot on a Sigma fp with middle grey anchored: Redlamp Neutral above, Redlamp Reproduction below. Under Reproduction its grey row reads 22.5, 35.5, 50.9, 66.4, 81.0 and 95.3, within 0.4 of published averages from Neutral 3.5 to 8.
