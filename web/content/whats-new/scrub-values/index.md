With the pointer over a value, a faint well and left-right arrows show it can be changed. Drag it left or right to move the setting across its range, with Shift for finer steps; the drag is one step in History, and a click still lets you type.

The colour wheels in Color Grading now show their Hue, Saturation and Luminance as values, the Tone Curve shows its split points and the selected point's Input and Output, and Base Look Amount takes typing, all of which you can type or drag.
