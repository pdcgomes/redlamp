Drag a mask or one of its components onto another to move it. Masks add up, so their order only tidies the list; components combine in order, so moving one can change its mask.

Image on B&W joins the overlay modes: what the mask covers stays in colour and the rest turns grey. The overlay's options now set its Opacity, for the modes that tint.
