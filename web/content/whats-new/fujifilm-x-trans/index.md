Redlamp now develops the raws of Fujifilm's X-Trans cameras with Markesteijn's demosaic, ported from LibRaw. It resolves finer detail, and fine lines and patterns no longer pick up green and magenta fringes. In the picture, solar panels from an X-T5 at 200%: 0.2.6 on the left, 0.2.7 on the right.

It applies to every X-Trans photo, so photos you've already edited look sharper too, with a little more of their grain where Luminance noise reduction is low.
