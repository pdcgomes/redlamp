In the Healing tool, choose Remove and set Fill to Generative. Content-Aware fills a spot from the photo around it; Generative repaints it with an image model, for things too large to fill from the photo, such as a parked car.

The first time, Redlamp downloads the model, FLUX.2 [klein] 4B, about 2.4 GB. It runs on this Mac, and your photos are never uploaded. Each spot gets three fills to choose from, labelled as generated. It needs a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more.
